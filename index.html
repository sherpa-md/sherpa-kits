How SherpaMD works

A Sherpa is readable Markdown—not an app by itself. A capable AI agent uses it as a bounded build and validation plan.

  1. Choose and check.Pick the outcome you want and read its verification badges. Draft and unverified work still needs proof.
  2. Give it to your AI.Select Use with AI, then paste the copied request into an agent that can inspect your environment and build software.
  3. Build safely and verify.Keep private data in an approved AI environment, approve sensitive actions, and require tests and evidence before accepting completion.
