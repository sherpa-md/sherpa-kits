# Sources and design inferences

- [OpenTelemetry traces](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/traces/) — defines correlated request paths, spans, timestamps, attributes, events, links, and statuses.
- [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) — provides entities, activities, agents, derivation, attribution, revision, and invalidation relationships.
- [NIST SP 800-218 Secure Software Development Framework](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-218) — authoritative secure-development practice framework used to shape remediation and recurrence controls for software incidents.

## Inferences

- Trace and provenance models can unify heterogeneous incident evidence, but they do not prove causality by themselves.
- The strongest AI role is evidence organization, hypothesis generation, and challenge; final causal acceptance belongs to a qualified human investigator.
