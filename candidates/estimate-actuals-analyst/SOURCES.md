# Sources and design inferences

- [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1) — governance and measurement framing for trustworthy AI use.
- [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) — provenance model for estimates, revisions, activities, sources, and derived adjustment artifacts.
- [Microsoft: Enterprise data protection in Copilot](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise-data-protection) — concrete approved-tenant implementation reference for organizations using Microsoft Copilot.

## Inferences

- Prospective improvement on later work is stronger evidence than a good retrospective fit.
- Separating scope change, incomplete actuals, and information available at estimate time is essential to avoid a misleading “AI found the estimator's bias” story.
