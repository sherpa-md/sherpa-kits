# Sources and design inferences

- [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) — standard vocabulary for entities, activities, agents, derivation, attribution, source, time, and revision.
- [OpenTelemetry traces](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/traces/) — model for correlated units of work, parent/child relationships, timestamps, events, links, attributes, and status.
- [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1) — risk-management framing for trustworthy deployment and evaluation.

## Inferences

- Provenance and trace concepts adapt well to operational reconciliation even when the underlying system is not software telemetry.
- The LLM is valuable for mapping and hypothesis generation; arithmetic and baseline/version control should remain deterministic.
